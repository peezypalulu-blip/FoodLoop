## Vision

PLATERIPPLE aims to allow approved home cooks, pudding and dessert makers, bakers, small caterers and restaurants — including people without websites or their own apps — to reach nearby buyers. Restaurants can propose one-hour lunch and dinner Rescue Drops, optionally extending an hour at up to R20 more while food is safe. Donations remain free.
