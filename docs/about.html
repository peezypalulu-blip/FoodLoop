Phenyo Lumatha —
Founder of FoodLoop

Phenyo Lumatha is the founder behind FoodLoop, an independent food-community project in South Africa. FoodLoop's long-term direction is to bring local food sellers and their communities closer together while encouraging more thoughtful use of food.

Why FoodLoop?

The FoodLoop idea starts with a simple belief: good food and local talent deserve better opportunities. The project is being developed with neighbourhood food experiences and independent sellers in mind—including cooks and makers who do not have their own apps or online storefronts.

FoodLoop is in development. Its planned marketplace, food offers and other features are not yet available for real orders. Any future food sellers, payments, locations or delivery services will be subject to practical, legal and safety requirements before launch.

A project with its own identity

FoodLoop is an independent project founded by Phenyo Lumatha. It is not the same application as Horizon Origin and has no publicly announced restaurant or delivery partners at this stage.

This is the official pre-launch founder page for FoodLoop. No restaurant, financial or service partnership is implied by this page.
